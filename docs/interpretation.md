# Interpretation Guide

The results represent a comparison of two analytical procedures applied to the same generated ordinal dataset.

CFI and TLI describe comparative fit, while RMSEA and SRMR describe different aspects of model misfit.

Chi-square should be interpreted together with other fit information.

Convergence and parameter estimates should be examined before interpreting model fit.

The polychoric correlation-based approach does not reproduce all components of the WLSMV estimation procedure. Therefore, differences should be interpreted as differences between analytical approaches rather than as evidence of universal superiority of one method.
